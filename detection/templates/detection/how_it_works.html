Workflow Process

Simple Steps

Our intelligent botanical recognition system makes identifying plants effortless through a streamlined 4-step workflow.

1
1. Upload Image

Upload or drag-and-drop a photo of any leaf, flower, or plant specimen from your device or webcam.

2
2. Process Image

OpenCV standardizes the image format, converts color to RGB, resizes to 224x224, and normalizes pixel intensities.

3
3. AI Model

TensorFlow & Keras deep neural network extracts morphological features and computes classification probabilities.

4
4. Get Result

Receive instant plant name, Latin scientific binomial, biological family, confidence percentage, and care details.
