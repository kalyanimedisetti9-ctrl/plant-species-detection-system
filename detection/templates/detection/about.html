Project Overview

About The Project

A Diploma-level academic project implementing computer vision and deep learning techniques to automate plant species recognition and taxonomic retrieval.

Academic Research

Automated Botanical Identification

The Plant Species Detection System is an intelligent web platform designed to streamline botanical classification using state-of-the-art computer vision and deep learning architectures.

Manual identification of plants from leaves and flora often demands specialized taxonomic knowledge and extensive reference manuals. By leveraging OpenCV image preprocessing and TensorFlow/Keras convolutional neural networks, this platform provides immediate species identification along with Latin binomial scientific names, biological families, and cultivation properties.

Developed as a practical Diploma project demonstration highlighting end-to-end machine learning pipelines integrated with a full-stack Django backend.

Mission & Objectives

Our Goals

Core academic and practical objectives targeted by the project.

Identify Plant Species

Accurately identify plant species directly using leaf and flora photographs with minimal human intervention.

Provide Fast & Accurate Results

Deliver real-time classification confidence and botanical profiles in under one second.

Help Students & Researchers

Serve as an interactive educational aid for students studying botany, taxonomy, and agricultural sciences.

Support Agricultural Applications

Assist farmers, agriculturalists, and gardeners in distinguishing crops, beneficial flora, and medicinal herbs.

Make Plant Identification Easier

Provide a clean, user-friendly responsive web platform accessible on smartphones, tablets, and computers.
